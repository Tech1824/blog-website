Common Questions

How do I become an author?
Register for a free account and contact the admin to request author access. We review all applications within 48 hours.
Can I delete my account?
Yes — go to Settings → Delete Account. This action is permanent and removes all your data.
How do I report inappropriate content?
Use this contact form with the subject "Content / Post Issue" and include the post link. We'll review it promptly.
Is BlogNest free to use?
Absolutely. Reading, writing, and interacting with posts is completely free for all members.
